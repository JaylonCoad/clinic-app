Thank you for opening my Clinic App!
This app allows full CRUD operations on Patients, Physicians, and Appointments.
There are also a variety of cool features I added, such as in-line buttons, sort by birthday for Patients/sort by date and time for Appointments, a "room" feature to only allow appointments to happen in one room at once, and the completion of appointments, which grays out that specific appointment in the list view.
Here are some rules to keep in mind:
1. When creating an Appointment, you must first have at least one Patient and one Physician created. Then, navigate to Patient View and click "Create Appointment", which will navigate you to select a Physician, and click "Create Appointment" once more.
2. Neither a Patient, Physician, nor Room can ever be double-booked.
3. Appointments can be booked 1 week in advance at the earliest and 4 weeks at the latest, and they must be scheduled on a weekday.
4. Appointments can only be booked at the top of the hour, from 8AM to 4PM, meaning times like 8:45AM / 9:20AM / 1:30PM etc are not allowed.
